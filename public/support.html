Frequently asked

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. This permanently deletes your profile, ratings, reviews, posts, reels, photos, messages, and sign-in record from our servers. It cannot be undone. If you can no longer sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

I can't sign in

Gourmet Canvas supports Sign in with Apple, Google, and email. If a sign-in fails, check that you're using the same method you signed up with. For email accounts, use the password reset link on the sign-in screen. Still stuck? Email us and we'll sort it out.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Location is used to show nearby restaurants, distances, and travel times. It's only accessed while you're using the app, and you can decline — search and most features work without it. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Gourmet Canvas.

Who can see my ratings and posts?

You control visibility when you post: content can be public or followers-only, and your follower list is approval-based. Direct messages are visible only to the people in the conversation.

How do I report a bug or a problem with content?

Email us with a screenshot and a short description. For content concerns, include a link or the username involved and we'll review it promptly.

How is my data handled?

The short version: your data is yours, we don't sell it, and there are no ads. The full details are in our privacy policy.
