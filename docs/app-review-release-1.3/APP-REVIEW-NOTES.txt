GoodEats version 1.3 is free. No subscription purchase is required, and subscriptions are not offered in this build. The draft subscriptions in App Store Connect are not part of this submission.

AI features are optional. Open the floating assistant, enter a message, and send it to see the explicit Anthropic/OpenAI data-sharing disclosure. Declining keeps the rest of the app available. Permission can be revoked in Profile → Settings → Privacy & permissions → Turn off AI sharing.

Community content and profiles have a three-dot Report or block menu. Blocked accounts can be managed in Privacy & permissions. When publishing new shared content or edits, a separate disclosure asks permission for OpenAI to screen shared text, photos and sampled video frames. Allowing this check can approve supported content automatically. Choosing Manual review keeps it private until a moderator reviews it. Flagged or unsupported content, including videos with audio, also requires manual review. Review status appears directly on the affected content. Remembered screening permission can be cleared in Privacy & permissions → Ask before each safety check.

Account deletion is available in Profile → Settings → Account & security → Delete account.
